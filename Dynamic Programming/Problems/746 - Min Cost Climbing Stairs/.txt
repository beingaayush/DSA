You are given an integer array cost where:
- cost[i] = cost of stepping on the i-th stair.
- After paying the cost, you can climb 1 or 2 stairs.
- You can start from index 0 or index 1.
- Your goal is to reach the top of the staircase.
- The top is one step beyond the last index.
Return the minimum cost needed to reach the top.
Example
cost = [10, 15, 20]

Answer = 15

One optimal way:
Start at index 1
Pay 15
Jump 2 steps → reach the top

Total = 15

Important: Reaching the top does not require paying a cost for the top itself.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🪜 Question actually kya bol raha hai?
Maan le:
cost = [10, 15, 20]

Iska matlab staircase:
       TOP
        ↑
   [20]  ← stair 2
   [15]  ← stair 1
   [10]  ← stair 0

Har stair par ek cost hai:
- Stair 0 → ₹10
- Stair 1 → ₹15
- Stair 2 → ₹20
Tum kya kar sakte ho?
Har baar:
- 1 stair aage ja sakte ho
- 2 stairs aage ja sakte ho
Aur jis stair par land karoge, uski cost pay karni padegi.
Example
cost = [10, 15, 20]

Tum stair 0 ya stair 1 se start kar sakte ho.
Option 1
Start → stair 0 → stair 1 → stair 2 → TOP

Cost:
10 + 15 + 20 = 45

Option 2
Start → stair 0 → stair 2 → TOP

Cost:
10 + 20 = 30

Option 3
Start → stair 1 → TOP

Cost:
15

So minimum:
15

Aisi path choose karo jisme stairs ki total cost minimum ho aur TOP tak pahunch jao.
Bas. Question mein stairs climb karne ka minimum cost nikalna hai.

Core idea : 
Har stair ke liye socho:
"Agar mujhe is stair tak pahunchna hai, toh minimum kitna cost lagega?"

Maan lo:
cost = [10, 15, 20]

Hum dp[i] ka matlab rakhenge:
dp[i] = stair i tak pahunchne ka minimum cost

Stair i par kaise aa sakte ho?
Sirf 2 ways:
i-1 → i       (1 step)
i-2 → i       (2 steps)

Toh dono mein se cheaper wala:
dp[i] = cost[i] + min(dp[i-1], dp[i-2])

Example
cost = [10, 15, 20]

dp[0] = 10
dp[1] = 15

dp[2] = 20 + min(15, 10)
     = 30

Lekin TOP n index par hai, aur top tak hum n-1 ya n-2 stair se aa sakte hain.
So final:
answer = min(dp[n-1], dp[n-2])

For [10,15,20]:
min(dp[2], dp[1])
= min(30, 15)
= 15

Bas recognition yaad rakh
1 ya 2 steps → previous 2 states → min()
dp[i] = cost[i] + min(dp[i-1], dp[i-2])

Yahi poora DP idea hai.

Final Idea:
Maan le:
cost = [10, 15, 20, 25]

Stairs:
stair 0     stair 1     stair 2     stair 3
 10           15           20           25
  🧍

Hum stair 3 par pahunchne ka minimum cost nikal rahe hain.
Stair 3 par kaise aa sakte ho?
Tum ek baar mein sirf 1 ya 2 stairs jump kar sakte ho.
Isliye stair 3 par aane ke sirf 2 possible ways hain:
Way 1:
stair 2 ──1 step──> stair 3

Way 2:
stair 1 ──2 steps──> stair 3

Bas. Isi ko i-1 aur i-2 bol rahe hain.
Ab cost samajh
Suppose:
dp[2] = 30

Matlab:
Stair 2 tak pahunchne mein minimum ₹30 laga.

Aur:
dp[1] = 15

Matlab:
Stair 1 tak pahunchne mein minimum ₹15 laga.

Ab stair 3 par jaana hai.
Way 1: Stair 2 se
stair 2 → stair 3

₹30 + stair 3 ki cost ₹25
= ₹55

Way 2: Stair 1 se
stair 1 → stair 3

₹15 + stair 3 ki cost ₹25
= ₹40

Obviously ₹40 cheaper hai.
Isliye:
dp[3] = 25 + min(dp[2], dp[1])
      = 25 + min(30, 15)
      = 40

Ab formula ko human language mein padho
dp[i] = cost[i] + min(dp[i-1], dp[i-2]);

Iska matlab:
Stair i par pahunchne ke liye, i-1 ya i-2 se aa sakte hain. Jo route pehle se cheaper hai, uska cost lo, aur current stair i ki cost add kar do.
