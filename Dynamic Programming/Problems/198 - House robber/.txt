Tumhare paas houses ki array hai:
nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]

Har house me kuch money hai.
Rule: Tum do adjacent houses ko rob nahi kar sakte.
Matlab agar house i rob kiya, toh i+1 nahi kar sakte.
Goal: maximum money steal karna hai.
Example:
2  7  9  3  1
↑     ↑     ↑
2 + 9 + 1 = 12
Answer = 12
---------------------------------------------------------

Core Idea
Har house i par tumhare paas 2 choices hain:

1. House rob nahi karo
Toh previous house tak jo maximum paisa mila tha, wahi rahega:
dp[i-1]

2. House rob karo
Toh previous house i-1 ko rob nahi kar sakte.
Isliye:
nums[i] + dp[i-2]

Dono me maximum:
dp[i] = max(dp[i-1], nums[i] + dp[i-2])

Bas yehi poora DP hai. Human civilization ne isko bhi interview question bana diya hai.
Example
nums = [2, 7, 9, 3, 1]

DP:
dp[0] = 2
dp[1] = max(2, 7) = 7

dp[2] = max(7, 2 + 9)
      = 11

dp[3] = max(11, 7 + 3)
      = 11

dp[4] = max(11, 11 + 1)
      = 12

so :- [2, 7, 11, 11, 12]
Answer = 12
